Use: SPACE (PC) or the SKILL button (mobile). Recharge: 10 seconds.

Controls & items
  • PC: WASD move, mouse aim, hold left click to shoot
  • V switch camera: top-down, third-person, first-person (VIEW button on mobile). In 3rd/1st person click to capture the mouse, move it to turn, Esc to release.
  • M mute or unmute sound (SOUND button at the top). Sounds from other players are placed left/right by position.
  • R reload (takes time, progress bar under you)
  • F use a medkit (+40 HP, you carry up to 5)
  • Spectate: Q/E or arrow keys after you die
  • Mobile: left stick move, right stick aim, FIRE / SKILL / RELOAD / MEDKIT buttons
  • Crates: border colour = rarity. Grey common, blue rare, purple epic, gold legendary. Weapons get +15/30/50% damage; armor and medkits give more.
  • Storm: stay inside the blue circle, it shrinks and hurts.
  • Ghost powers: when eliminated, tap Become a Ghost. Spectate a survivor and cast Meteor [1], Gift [2] or Reveal [3]. Land 2 meteor hits on survivors to come back to life!
  • Bounty: 2 kills make you WANTED (gold crown, always on the minimap). Killing a WANTED player gives +50 HP and a legendary drop.
  • Radar: the minimap only shows enemies that are close. Aim at an enemy and your crosshair turns red.
